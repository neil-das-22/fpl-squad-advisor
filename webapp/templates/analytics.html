What predicts FPL points -- beyond the obvious?

Goals, assists, clean sheets and bonus points obviously drive points -- that's not interesting. This asks a sharper question: among the underlying quality-of-play stats (ICT sub-indices, expected output, defensive actions, price, ownership), which ones actually track a player's season points total, position by position? Correlation strength (r) runs from −1 (perfectly inverse) to +1 (perfectly in step); anything past ±0.7 is a strong real-world relationship.
